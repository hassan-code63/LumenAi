# ✦ Lumen

### Every AI model, in one beautiful place.

**Lumen** is a thoughtfully curated directory of AI tools designed to make discovering and exploring artificial intelligence simpler, faster, and more enjoyable.

Instead of jumping between endless websites, searching for the right assistant, or keeping track of dozens of AI platforms, Lumen brings a hand-picked collection of powerful AI tools together in one clean, elegant experience.

Less searching. More creating.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://lumenai.pages.dev

---

## ✨ What is Lumen?

Lumen is a lightweight, user-friendly gateway to some of the most interesting AI platforms available today.

Whether you're writing, coding, researching, generating images, or simply exploring what AI can do, Lumen helps you find the right tool for the task and get started with a single click.

No complicated onboarding. No unnecessary steps. Just discover, choose, and create.

## 🚀 Features

- **Curated AI Collection** — Discover a hand-picked selection of 12 AI platforms.
- **Smart Discovery** — Find tools using the built-in search and category filters.
- **One-Click Access** — Open each tool's official website directly.
- **Multiple Categories** — Explore tools for chat, coding, research, writing, and image generation.
- **Favorites** — Save the AI tools you use most and access them easily.
- **Clean Experience** — Enjoy a minimal interface designed to keep your attention on what matters.
- **No Lumen Account Required** — Browse and explore without creating an account.
- **Privacy-Conscious Design** — Your conversations happen directly on the providers' platforms, not on Lumen.

## 🔒 Privacy & Transparency

Lumen is designed to be a simple gateway, not another place where your conversations are collected.

- Conversations take place on the official websites of the selected AI providers.
- Lumen does not operate a chat backend.
- Favorite models are saved locally in your browser.
- No Lumen account is needed to browse the collection.

Please note that external AI providers have their own privacy policies and account requirements.

## 💡 How It Works

Three simple steps:

1. **Browse** — Explore the curated collection.
2. **Choose** — Find the model that matches your needs.
3. **Chat** — Open its official platform and start exploring.

That's it. No unnecessary complexity.
